WHO WE ARE

Making Student Accommodation Simple & Easy

Finding a suitable place to stay can be difficult for students, especially when they move to a new city for education.

Student Stay Lodge helps students discover nearby lodges, rooms and flats in one place. Students can check important information such as rent, room type and available facilities before choosing a place to stay.

Our goal is to make the search for student accommodation easier, faster and more convenient.

THE PROBLEM

Why Did We Create Student Stay Lodge?
